# Pflanzenillustration

Erstellt mit dem integrierten Bildgenerierungswerkzeug. Die PNG-Datei ist zusätzlich als Data-URL in `index.html` eingebettet, damit die Lernumgebung als einzelne Datei offline nutzbar bleibt.

## Prompt

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: small illustration for Wortweise, a German-language vocabulary learning website. Primary request: a small young plant growing upward, symbolizing a growth mindset and learning through practice. Subject: one delicate seedling with a gently curved stem, several fresh green leaves and a tiny new leaf at the top, rooted in a small natural mound of earth. Style: warm, refined hand-painted watercolor and colored pencil illustration, simple readable silhouette, friendly for schoolchildren, not anthropomorphic. Palette: sage and forest greens matching website colors #286c50 and #e7f1e9, warm brown earth. Composition: centered square, whole plant visible with comfortable margins, suitable for display at 140 pixels. Background: genuinely transparent alpha, no rectangular backdrop. No text, no lettering, no watermark, no border.
