# Kindliches Pflanzensymbol

Mit dem integrierten Bildgenerierungswerkzeug aus `growth-plant.png` erstellt. Gespeichert als `growth-plant-childlike.png` und direkt in `index.html` eingebettet.

## Prompt

Use case: style-transfer. Edit the referenced plant illustration for a children's vocabulary learning website. Keep the core concept: a little green seedling growing from a small brown earth mound, centered and entirely visible, symbolizing growth through learning. Make it considerably more childlike and playful: simple rounded plump leaves, a gently curved thick stem, soft bold clean outlines, cheerful fresh green colors, minimal flat shading, charming children's picture-book sticker style. Replace realistic veins and rough earth texture with very simple smooth shapes. Add a tiny friendly smiling face on one leaf, subtle and clear. No text, no extra objects, no watermark. Truly transparent background with alpha, no white rectangle. Square composition with comfortable margins, readable at 88–140 pixels.
